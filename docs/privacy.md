# SnoreLog Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 24 November 2026

SnoreLog ("the app") is made by **[DEVELOPER NAME]** ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app uses, where it is kept, and the choices you have.

## The short version

- SnoreLog records and analyzes sound **only on your iPhone**. Audio is never uploaded.
- We do not run servers, and we do not collect, receive, sell or share any of your information.
- There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party SDKs in the app.
- Apple Health access is optional and **read-only**. Health data stays on your iPhone.
- Purchases of SnoreLog Pro are handled entirely by Apple.

## Microphone and audio

When you start a night, SnoreLog uses the microphone to record sound while you sleep. The background audio mode keeps recording while the screen is locked.

In the morning, the recording is analyzed on your iPhone with Apple's built-in sound recognition (the SoundAnalysis framework) to find snoring. No audio, and nothing derived from it, is sent to us or to anyone else.

After analysis:

- SnoreLog keeps short clips of the most notable snoring episodes (up to 30 clips per night, each up to about 20 seconds) so you can listen back.
- The full recording is deleted, unless you turn on **Settings > Keep full recordings**.
- Audio files are marked as excluded from iCloud and computer backups, so they stay on the device only.

## Night data

For each night, SnoreLog stores on your iPhone:

- start and end time, the snore score, snoring time and loudness levels;
- the list of snoring episodes (time, length, loudness);
- tags you choose, such as "Alcohol" or "Nasal strip";
- if you use Apple Health, the daily totals described below.

This data lives in the app's private storage on your device. Like other app data, it may be included in your iPhone's own backups (iCloud Backup or a computer backup) if you have those turned on. Those backups are controlled by you and Apple. We cannot see them.

## Apple Health (optional)

If you turn on **Settings > Use Apple Health**, SnoreLog asks for permission to **read** the following from the day before each night:

- workouts (including strength training)
- steps
- water
- caffeine
- alcoholic drinks

SnoreLog uses these only to show, in your reports, what might have affected your snoring. It saves the daily totals with each night on your iPhone.

SnoreLog **never writes** to Apple Health. Health data is **never** sent off your device, never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, and never shared with third parties. It is not used for any purpose other than showing you your own reports.

You choose which types SnoreLog can read, and you can change that at any time in the Health app (tap your profile picture, then Apps, then SnoreLog). Turning **Use Apple Health** off in SnoreLog also removes the Health totals it has saved.

## PDF report

SnoreLog Pro can create a PDF report of your nights. The PDF is made on your iPhone. It only leaves your device if you choose to share it, for example by email, AirDrop or printing. Once shared, it is handled by the app or person you sent it to.

## Purchases

SnoreLog Pro is sold through Apple's App Store using in-app purchases and subscriptions. Payment is handled by Apple. We never receive your name, payment details or Apple ID. The app checks with Apple's StoreKit, on your device, whether Pro is unlocked. Apple may share anonymous, aggregated sales reports with us as part of normal App Store operation. Apple's handling of your information is covered by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

You can manage or cancel a subscription in **Settings > SnoreLog Pro > Manage subscription**, or in your Apple ID settings.

## Crash reports

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings (Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements), Apple may send us anonymous crash reports. These help us fix bugs and do not identify you. SnoreLog itself does not include any crash-reporting or analytics code.

## Deleting your data

Everything SnoreLog stores is on your iPhone, so you are in control:

- Swipe to delete a night in **History**. This deletes its data and audio.
- Use **Settings > Delete all nights** to remove every night and recording.
- Deleting the app removes all of its data from your iPhone.

Because we do not hold any of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

## Children

SnoreLog is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

## Not a medical device

SnoreLog is not a medical device and does not diagnose sleep apnea or any other condition. If you are worried about your sleep or breathing, talk to a doctor.

## Changes to this policy

If the app starts handling information differently, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above before that version of the app is released.
